Agentic Experience Is an Operating Model

The experience of agency is shaped by the operating model around an AI system as much as by its interface.

Agentic Experience / Product Strategy / AIReading time to be confirmed

An agent can plan, choose tools, and act across several steps. That changes the design question from what should happen after a click to what the system is allowed to decide, how people stay informed, and how a mistaken action can be recovered.

Agentic experience therefore extends beyond chat. It includes authority, context, progress, approval, memory, failure, and accountability.

Essay seed

This entry is a structured writing seed, not a published article. Confirm examples, claims, date, and reading time before publication.

01

Authority

Every action needs a clear permission boundary.

The interface should distinguish what the agent can inspect, propose, change, or send. The higher the consequence, the more explicit the user's control and the recovery path need to be.

02

Legibility

Progress is part of trust.

People need to understand the goal, current step, evidence used, remaining uncertainty, and points where their judgment is required without reading an internal reasoning trace.

03

Recovery

A useful agent knows how to stop.

Undo, approval, bounded retries, escalation, and safe failure are core interactions. Agency without recovery is simply automation with a larger blast radius.
