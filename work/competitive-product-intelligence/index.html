Product strategy

Competitive product intelligence

Turning software benchmarking into a repeatable product decision tool rather than a one-off comparison exercise.

JourneyReviewPatternPriority

Context

Enterprise software teams needed a clearer view of how their workflows compared with the market and where experience improvements would matter.

Challenge

Feature checklists alone could not explain the quality of a workflow or connect observations to customer needs and product strategy.

Decision

Create a reusable evaluation method that combines customer journeys, expert review, and structured comparison.

System

Compared workflows across consistent dimensions, then organized observations into patterns, experience gaps, and strategic themes so product teams could discuss priorities with a common evidence base.

Evidence

The method helped turn competitive research into a recurring product strategy input, with findings that could be revisited as the portfolio evolved.
