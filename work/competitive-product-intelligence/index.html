Leadership case

Competitive product intelligence

Building a repeatable evaluation method that turns competitor workflows into product-strategy input and prioritized opportunities.

MapCompareEvaluatePrioritize
ScopeEnterprise software
RoleFramework lead
FocusProduct-strategy input

Context

Complex software teams needed a clearer way to learn from competing products without reducing the exercise to a feature checklist. The useful questions were about workflows, user expectations, trade-offs, and where product decisions could create stronger value.

Challenge

Competitive reviews can become disconnected collections of screenshots and observations. Without a consistent method, the findings are difficult to compare, difficult to hand over, and easy to lose when delivery priorities change.

Decision

Create a structured evaluation framework that maps comparable workflows, examines experience patterns, captures the reasoning behind observations, and translates findings into product opportunities.

System

The method combines workflow decomposition, pattern comparison, evidence capture, opportunity framing, stakeholder review, and prioritization. It was designed so additional designers could continue the work instead of relying on one expert.

Leadership value

The framework made competitive intelligence useful as a product-strategy input. It also created a repeatable learning method that could move between products and designers.

Outcomes and value

What this work changed
