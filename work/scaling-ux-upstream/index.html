Scaling UX upstream

Moving experience design from late-stage review into the product decisions that shape complex enterprise software.

EvidenceBriefPrototypeBuild

Context

A global industrial technology portfolio was growing its software capability across multiple product teams.

Challenge

UX often entered after implementation decisions had already been made, leaving little room to validate assumptions or reduce rework risk.

Decision

Shift the conversation upstream. Introduce structured discovery, clear design briefs, early prototypes, and customer validation before development commitment.

System

Built repeatable quality gates, shared practices, coaching routines, and portfolio visibility so the approach could work beyond one product team.

Evidence

The UX capability expanded from 2 to 28 people across domains, with a clearer role in discovery and product planning.
