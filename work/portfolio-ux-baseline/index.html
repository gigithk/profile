Leadership case

A portfolio UX baseline

Turning fragmented UX activity into a shared view of maturity, priorities, and quality controls across a software portfolio.

DiscoverDesignValidateDeliver
ScopeSoftware portfolio
RoleFramework owner
ModelFour lifecycle stages

Context

A growing product portfolio needed a practical way to understand which UX practices were already in place, where the important gaps remained, and which improvements should be prioritized next.

Challenge

Without a common baseline, teams could discuss UX quality in abstract terms but could not compare practices consistently. Discovery, design, validation, and delivery activities existed at different levels of maturity across products.

Decision

Create a lightweight portfolio baseline organized around four lifecycle stages: discover, design, validate, and deliver. Keep the language operational so teams can use it in planning discussions instead of treating it as a separate assessment exercise.

System

The baseline covers persona and segment understanding, journey mapping, stakeholder alignment, information architecture, design-system alignment, workflows, prototyping, UI review, usability testing, analytics, documentation, handoff, and effort tracking.

Leadership value

The baseline gives product and UX leaders a shared language for improvement. It turns a broad maturity conversation into specific practices that can be discussed, sequenced, and supported.

Outcomes and value

What this work changed

Shared portfolio view

Teams gained a consistent way to discuss UX practices across the lifecycle.

Actionable improvement areas

The framework makes gaps visible without exposing confidential product-level status publicly.

Playbook direction

The baseline informed practical guidance for review, handoff, research, testing, analytics, and estimation.

Value: A visible baseline helps leadership invest in the right improvements and helps product teams understand the next practical step without adding heavy process.

DesignOps operating model

Public summary. Product-level maturity status, adoption details, and internal screenshots remain private.
